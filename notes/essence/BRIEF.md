## 1. The mandate in one paragraph

One process keeps appearing wherever local information becomes global truth: **forget one piece (a conditional expectation onto everything except that piece), re-randomise or reconstruct it, and let the repetition converge to global mixing, recovery or approximation**, with the rate set by an angle between "forget this" and "forget that". The JSV permanent chain (remove an edge, slide a hole, re-add), the Jeronimo–Mittal–Roy "ignore the first step" recursion for expander walks, Glauber dynamics with spectral independence, the matroid down–up walk (drop i = ∂ᵢ), localization schemes, Gaussian elimination as Schur complement plus sparse resampling, Sinkhorn and operator scaling, cluster mutation, tiling flips with coupling from the past, and Jones' tower all share it. Each team goes **deep** into specific existences of this process in its domain, especially those with **large computational or complexity payoff**, finds the **noncommutative (NCG) generalisation** (what is known, and what is the precise next theorem), and only then asks what the process becomes for our problem. The theory drives the design. The competition is where the theory is tested, quickly at small width and properly at n = 1024 with adjusted MSE.

## 2. The anti-naivety rule

The user's critique (verbatim in the input file): our thinking has been "implicitly and naively tied to the standard current frontier approaches", drawing "each layer's neurons" as the graph of interest. **Do not default to that drawing.** For every transfer you propose, say explicitly which object plays each role (the pieces, the down step, the up step, the angle, the global quantity) and why it is not the per-neuron, per-layer picture in disguise. Candidate objects, none mandatory, others welcome:

1. **Wick pairings and hafnians.** For jointly Gaussian variables E[∏ gᵢ] = haf(Cov), a sum over perfect matchings. Expanding every gate in Hermite polynomials writes the network mean as a weighted sum of hafnians of propagated covariances. Pieces are pairing edges; "remove one" is a hole or cavity ratio; pseudorandom weights may make the ratios concentrate.
2. **Path systems.** a_{L,c}(x) is a sum over input-to-output paths of weight products times gates. Moments pair paths; non-Gaussianity lives in collisions (paths through a shared neuron); old content is paths that split at a birth and are contracted many layers later.
3. **The Ornstein–Uhlenbeck / Wiener-chaos decomposition of the input law.** The noise operator P_ρ is itself a down–up process with angle ρ = cos θ. The two-replica kernel K_l(ρ) = E[a_l(x) a_l(x′)ᵀ] with corr(x, x′) = ρ runs from the second moment (ρ = 1, local) to m mᵀ (ρ = 0, global).
4. **The weight filtration.** With F_l = σ(W_1, …, W_l), any estimator's error splits into orthogonal martingale increments, one per layer. This is why the books close as an incoherent sum over layers (region §3). Fresh weights make the next layer's weight algebra and the current state's algebra a commuting square.
5. **The gate algebra.** Each gate pattern is a diagonal projection; a gated propagator is an alternating product of projections and Gaussian matrices. Two-projection angle laws (the arcsine law for free projections of trace ½) set its spectrum (participation ratio ≈ n/(2·age), foundations-facts F9.1).
6. **The activation fan.** With no biases every linear region is a polyhedral cone, and the network refines a random conical tessellation of the sphere layer by layer. The mean is a sum over cones of a linear map times the cone's Gaussian first moment: conic intrinsic volumes, angle sums, deletion–restriction.
7. **The diagram poset.** Births (second-chaos creations at each ReLU) and their transports and contractions form a graded poset, like a Bratteli diagram, over which the moment chain sums.
8. **The dilation sector.** Positive homogeneity a_l(λx) = λ a_l(x) makes the scale-mixing law an exactly conserved charge of every arrow (costate C6). Its variance grows linearly in depth, a unipotent (critical) signature, and He-initialised ReLU sits exactly at criticality: the correlation map has slope 1 at ρ = 1.
9. **The input-space Hessian field.** The second-chaos coefficients H^c_t = E_x[∇²_x z_{t,c}(x)] and first-chaos Γ_t = Cov(x, z_t) live in one fixed basis (input space) shared by all layers; the third-cumulant slices are contractions of H with Γ.

The programme's standing constraints still hold: objects are abstract barycentres, not activation vectors; an arrow is a linear layer with the ReLU immediately before it; general theory must not be dragged into niche specifics; a negative result is charged first to the dictionary.

## 3. The problem and what is established (do not re-derive; cite)

- **Task.** Estimate the per-neuron post-ReLU means of the final layer of random He-initialised, bias-free ReLU MLPs (width 1024, depth 16; smoke test 256 × 32), input x ~ N(0, I). Score = mean final-layer MSE × max(0.1, F/B), B = 2⁴¹ FLOPs. One unit u = one dense 1024³ float32 product = 2³¹ FLOPs, so B = 1024 u.
- **The bar.** J2W adjusted 1.6e-9 (raw ≈ 1.5e-8 at 0.11 B, i.e. ≈ 7 u per layer); marius_binner raw 1.14e-8 at 0.15 B. The public moment chain: raw 2.1e-8 at 0.25 B (adjusted 5.4e-9). Grader limits: 120 s wall, 0.4 s residual, 8 GB.
- **Calibration.** The exact bivariate-Gaussian closure scores raw 4.1e-6 at n = 1024, ≈ 270× above the bar.
- **Best fresh estimator** (`notes/fresh-slate/breakthrough/region/REPORT.md`, "FC"): a first-order Wiener-chaos estimator with two-time cross-covariances, every age, exact slices and an O(n²) mean-field κ4 recursion scores **raw 3.03e-8 ± 0.35e-8 on all 6 bench networks**, within 2× of the leaders. It costs ≈ 840 u dense (≈ 0.5 B with Strassen if wall-feasible), so adjusted ≈ 1.5–2.5e-8. **Accuracy is reached; cost binds.**
- **The one binding object: memory.** About 40 % of each next (2,1) slice D21 is third-order content born at earlier ReLUs (foundations-facts F8.1). It is nearly orthogonal to the present and to other ages (F8.3); every age matters (dropping ages > 4 costs 25× the bar, region N6); it has ensemble mean zero, so it is purely quenched (F8.5). Every cheap carrier measured so far fails at n = 1024: windows, per-source row bases (k = 256: 4× worse), shared Oseledets subspaces (q = n/4: 6×), Wick-atom pruning (100×), slice-atom pruning (2.5×), symmetric Tucker, CP caps, rank ladders, Gaussian scale mixtures, learned corrections, MC control variates (the published chain's author lists 25 dead ends, `notes/digests/504aldo-k3-chain.md`). In any atom-by-atom (bilinear) realisation the all-age content costs ≥ 480 u (region §6; costate C3: Θ(L² n³) in every natural contraction order). **The leaders carry it at about zero cost**: their whole bill equals the public chain minus its old tier.
- **Structure known so far.** Fresh-weight lemma: Frobenius norms are the currency and errors add incoherently across layers (region §1). Loops with fresh-weight legs are negligible (signings). Coincident index patterns are contracted coherently by the next layer's paired fresh weights, so mean-gate transport is wrong at O(1) there (theory judge, `notes/fresh-slate/judge/theory.md` §2). At depth the global (2,2) spike is 95–97 % one strongly non-Gaussian collective coordinate along the Perron/mean direction (bethe). The joint fourth cumulant is needed only through two n-vectors per layer (region N7).
- **Where the facts are.** `notes/fresh-slate/foundations-facts.md` (numbered facts F1–F16), `notes/fresh-slate/CONVERGENCE.md`, `notes/fresh-slate/judge/{quant,theory}.md`, the breakthrough reports in `notes/fresh-slate/breakthrough/*/REPORT.md`.
