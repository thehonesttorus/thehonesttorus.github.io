## 1. The one-paragraph answer

**No. Nothing in this literature moves the leaderboard number, and the gap arithmetic says nothing in this *class* of literature could.** We are at 8.675e-7 raw / 8.67e-8 adjusted; the leader is 1.93e-8 raw, so we need **45x raw**. The single best-costed candidate the whole study produced is purification/factor propagation of the covariance (a Cholesky refactor per layer, 0.26% of budget) whose entire prize is capped by our own measurement at **2%** — obstruction 3, where a 1e-6 eigenvalue floor already captures it and 1e-4 actively hurts (5.183e-7 → 8.538e-7). Everything else is worse: a bivariate moment-PSD constraint at 0.005% of budget that cannot bind because our error points *into* the cone (we under-estimate κ₃ by 64% at layer 16, i.e. we are too Gaussian, i.e. strictly interior); an instance-universal truncation schedule (Rieffel Thm 8.2) that needs a compact group we do not have and, reduced to its transferable residue, is Fejér smoothing, which saves zero FLOPs; an exact Carathéodory reduction of the path sum that is post-hoc, non-transferable, and re-imports the 11x amplification through weight error. And the ceiling on the entire family is settled independently of any paper: the published oracle for **perfect marginals at every layer** is 7.07e-7, so a free, exact, level-infinity version of every per-layer state approximation in this corpus lands at **1.23x** from where we already sit — while the leader is **36.6x beyond that oracle**. Whatever the leader is doing, it is not a better marginal closure. That single fact invalidates the corpus at a stroke, because every method in all seven clusters is a per-object approximation with marginal or scalar-functional content.

## 2. The structural diagnosis, stated once

Six axes. Each is independently sufficient to kill a paper; the corpus fails on four or more, uniformly.

**(A) Approximating a fixed object in a limit vs. closing a dynamical propagation under a budget.** Every deliverable in the corpus is an existence, equivalence, classification, or convergence statement as N→∞, Λ→∞, or level→∞. There is no composition of maps anywhere in clusters 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 — no semigroup, no depth index, no error accumulation, no memory kernel. Fiore–Pisacane declare their reduction legitimate only under `PH = HP`, i.e. the retained sector is *exactly* dynamically invariant. They occupy the memoryless corner; we live entirely in its complement, and our Lemma 1 (`finite-resolution-question-algebra.md`) proves ReLU's chaos expansion has nonzero components at every even degree, so **no** finite resolution is closed under a layer.

**(B) A seminorm fixed a priori vs. one induced by future observables.** Their weighting comes from the object's own static geometry — `‖[D,a]‖`, a group length function, a commutator-Laplacian eigenvalue, singular-value decay, entropy Hessian. What we need is the pullback of the linear-response transport to layer 16 applied to a layer-ℓ perturbation, which is not a metric-geometric datum of layer ℓ at all. This is measured, not philosophical: re-ranking source units by `|p_β|·‖U_β‖^s` at s = 0/1/3 gives **6.844 / 6.791 / 6.772e-7** against exact 2.316e-7. The ranking moves the answer ~1%; the truncation costs 2.9x. Better a-priori mode orderings are a closed direction, and every construction in clusters 1–4 and 7 is one.

**(C) Norms are phase-blind; our target is a signed cancelling sum.** Every quantitative object in the corpus is a norm, a sup over a Lipschitz/unit ball, a unitarily invariant ideal functional, or a PSD condition. All are magnitude-selecting and sign-blind. Our coherence is **0.0887–0.1106** with sign agreement 0.506 against chance 0.500, so what a dual-ball extremal reconstructs is `Σ‖term‖`, which is ~11x inflated relative to the residue we need. Every bound of triangle-inequality or Cauchy–Schwarz shape — Kerr–Li Lemma 6.1's explicit factor `2n`, Anshu–Jekel–Landry's `2nl`, Xu et al. Thm 26, the trace-norm bound `|ω(A)−ω_L(A)| ≤ ‖ρ−σ_L‖₁‖A‖` — starts ~11x loose before it starts.

**(D) `PaP` is the corpus's universal construction, and we killed it four times.** Spectral truncation of the transport, the Cartan corner `1_Y A 1_Y`, symmetric rank-R diagonal memory, and polarised rank-one source modes all failed with the identical signature: immediate bias, then flat, and *non-monotone* (rank 16 at K0=3 scores 4.567e-7, **better** than rank 64 at 4.729e-7; polarised memory R=1024 at 2.018e-6 vs R=128 at 2.117e-6, an eightfold rank increase buying 4.7%). Latrémolière's only two concrete compression maps are literally our failed (a) and our failed (b). The corpus's constructive toolkit is drawn from the family we falsified.

**(E) Finite dimensions makes the theorems true and empty.** Instantiate on `M_1024`: all norms equivalent, every operator compact and trace class, every normed ideal is `B(H)`, `A'' = A` hyperfinite automatically, Voiculescu's `k_Φ ≡ 0`, every coordinate-subset projection trivially CP, a finite-dimensional operator system 1-exact and at `dist_op = 0` from a matrix algebra. Connes' Theorems 8/11/17/19 and Voiculescu 2.1/3.1 hold vacuously — Voiculescu 3.1 reduces to "0 is unitarily equivalent to 0". The only escape is the infinite-dimensional Ornstein–Uhlenbeck triple our own notes already construct, and that is a *readout* representation, capped by obstruction 4 at ~500x below our error.

**(F) Every quantitative success in the corpus is bought by symmetry we do not have.** D'Andrea's exact `d(Ψ_z,Ψ_z') = |z−z'|` needs a Heisenberg group with coherent-state orbits and a ladder Dirac; Fiore–Pisacane's counterterm is fixed by exact so(D+1) rep theory; Devi et al.'s collapse theorem needs transitive unitary action on trace-norm level sets; Aguilar–Kaad–Kyed's non-amplifying `β_N` needs `S²_q` to be a quantum homogeneous space; Rieffel's universal `δ_n` needs an ergodic compact-group action with faithful `π`. We are handed *the exact weights of one fixed instance*. Orthogonal invariance lives in the He ensemble, not the instance. The universality we do exploit (kernel coefficient across-network sd 0.00314 vs across-seed spread 0.031–0.040) is an empirical ensemble property; no argument flows from these papers to it.

**Triage rule for any future paper, derived from the corpus.** Ask three questions. (1) Does it contain an error functional that decreases in a computational parameter, with a *finite-parameter* rate? Across 42 papers: yes in three places only — Rieffel math/0011063 Thm 8.2, D'Andrea Lemma 6.9, and the NPA-style hierarchies. (2) Is that functional *signed*? Across 42 papers: **never**. (3) Does it price anything in operations? Essentially never — one qubit gate count, plus empirical SDP solver ceilings (n = 18–25), which is a scale report, not a cost model. Fail (2) and the paper cannot address our binding obstruction no matter how elegant.

## 3. What transfers, ranked

**The actionable list is empty.** Below are the five strongest near-misses, fully costed, so nobody re-derives them. Then the one live experiment the study surfaced, which is honestly *not* from this literature.

### 3.0 (LIVE, not from the corpus) Reweighted sparse re-summation of the source term bank

- **Provenance, stated plainly.** Surfaced by *contrast* while reading Flora et al. — whose search space is strictly binary include/exclude with fixed Hamming weight, i.e. term-preserving by construction and a subset of what we already falsified. The mathematical ancestor is Carathéodory (1911)/Steinitz (1913) and column-subset selection, both outside the corpus. Crediting any paper here would be exactly the fabricated verbal connection the provenance warning describes.
- **Construction.** From the age-1 exact source layer (already computed; 6.25% of budget, already spent) we hold the per-source-unit term bank `{T_β ∈ R^n}` whose sum is the exact contribution. Solve `min_{|S|=k, w} ‖Σ_{β∈S} w_β T_β − Σ_β T_β‖²` by OMP/NNLS. This drops terms **and re-solves the weights of survivors**, so the `√(fn)` injection model of obstruction 1 does not cover it.
- **In our notation.** A sparse, reweighted approximation of the *aggregate* `κ₃` path-sum contribution — obstruction 2's side of the line — rather than of the terms.
- **FLOPs at n=1024, L=16.** OMP to k = 64 over 1024 candidates: ~64 × 1024 × 1024 ≈ 6.7e7 = 0.003% of `2^41`. Applying the fitted `(S,w)` at ages 2..8: 64 transports × 7 ages × ~1e6 ≈ 4.5e8 = 0.02%. Thousands of subset evaluations are free.
- **Obstructions it must survive.** Obstruction 1 (evaded by reweighting) *and* obstruction 2's transfer requirement: support and weights must be network-independent or a function of `(t, σ, depth)`, else it is post-hoc.
- **The experiment that settles it.** Fit at age 1 on network 0; apply at ages 2..8; score against the exact depth-8 path sum; repeat the fit on networks 1–7 and test whether the support and weights coincide. If they do not transfer, the finding is worth having anyway: it closes obstruction 2 by showing the aggregate is compressible *only* through `(t,σ)`-measurable statistics — which is exactly the shipped kernel — and "no term-subset works" is upgraded from inference to measurement.

### 3.1 Purification / factor propagation — Freeman et al. 2206.13659 App. B.8; Becker–Li Bures Lyapunov map

- Carry `S` with `C = S Sᵀ` (Cholesky refactor per layer) instead of `C`, so the propagated covariance is PSD by construction.
- **FLOPs.** `n³/3 ≈ 3.58e8` × 16 ≈ 5.7e9 = **0.26%** of budget. Affordable (our whole covariance sandwich is 2.34%).
- **Obstruction 3.** Prize is capped at ~2% by direct measurement; worse, our single negative eigenvalue (−1e-4 of trace) originates *entirely* in the two-point cumulant correction — the channel worth 3.1x. Forcing that additive correction into a CP/density-operator form would most plausibly shrink or drop it, costing more than the 2% recovered. And the third-cumulant path sum is a perturbative correction, not a state; it does not embed in a density-operator picture at all.
- **Verdict: dead.** Settled by the floor sweep already in results.txt §7.

### 3.2 Bivariate Hamburger moment-matrix condition — d'Alessandro–Roch i Carceller–Tavakoli, reduced

- Impose the 6×6 PSD moment condition on each pair `(z_a, z_b)` from `μ`, `C_ab`, `K21_ab`. (The honest n-variable level-2 matrix has side 5.25e5, ~1.4e17 FLOPs, five orders over budget.)
- **FLOPs.** ~5e5 pairs × ~200 ≈ 1e8 = **0.005%** of budget. Free.
- **Obstruction 3 + obstruction 4.** Structurally evades obstruction 1 (constrains the aggregate tensor entry, not the cancelling terms), which is why it ranks here at all. It dies because a projection moves an estimate by at most its infeasibility: infeasibility is 1e-4 of trace against a **64% missing** third-cumulant sector, and truncating source depth *under*-estimates κ₃, moving the state toward Gaussian, which is strictly interior to the cone. A one-sided positivity constraint is inactive against excess Gaussianity.
- **Verdict: dead**, and capped a second time by the 7.07e-7 marginal oracle (1.23x).

### 3.3 Instance-universal truncation schedule — Rieffel math/0011063 Thm 8.2

- `dist_q(A, B_n) ≤ δ_n = ∫_G φ_n(x) l(x) dx`, `δ_n` depending only on `(π, l)`, not on `A` or `α`. Formally the object obstruction 1 says does not exist for us, proved by comparing a hard cutoff against a smoothed operator — formally our aggregate-vs-terms distinction.
- **In our notation.** Nothing: there is no group. The rules hand us one fixed instance; with no `G` there is no `χ`, no `φ_n`, no `B_n`. The bound works because the truncation index and the seminorm are the *same* grading, and we measured (obstruction 1, §B above) that no such alignment exists in any grading we have.
- **FLOPs.** The transferable residue is "taper instead of cut", i.e. Fejér 1904. A taper over all n singular directions saves **zero** compute — our spectral truncation exists only to buy budget — while a taper over the r retained modes strictly increases dropped mass. Excluded a priori by flatness: a Cesàro reweighting along a flat axis is a convex average of equal scores.
- **Verdict: dead as method, best-in-corpus as a framing device.** See reading list.

### 3.4 Exact Carathéodory/Steinitz reduction — cited in Zhang–Yu–Yu Thm 5

- Sign-absorbed, `Σ c_i v_i = S · Σ (|c_i|/S)[sgn(c_i) v_i]`, so our depth-8 path sum (~16,384 contributions in R¹⁰²⁴) is reproduced **exactly** by ≤1025 of them with re-solved nonnegative weights. Genuinely aggregate-preserving.
- **FLOPs.** Post-hoc: it takes the terms as *input*, and producing them is the entire cost (one exact source layer = 6.25%). Summing terms once produced is ~1.7e7 ≈ 0.0008%. It compresses the storage of a quantity we have already fully paid to compute.
- **Obstruction 1, re-entering through the back door.** Exactness is load-bearing: at coherence 0.089–0.111, `S/‖Σ‖ ≈ 11`, so a relative ε in the weights becomes ≈11ε in the answer. And the surviving subset is found by elimination on one network's realised terms; it transfers to no other network or layer.
- **Verdict: dead in exact form.** Its *approximate, transferable* form is item 3.0.

### 3.5 Norm-preserving rescale after spectral truncation — derived from D'Andrea Prop. 5.1

- Prop. 5.1 makes the resolution floor a function of `‖D_Λ‖`, not `rank(P_Λ)`, predicting that rescaling retained rank-R modes to restore the discarded operator norm beats plain energy truncation.
- **FLOPs.** Free (rank-256 transport is 0.39%, the rescale is O(1)).
- **Obstruction 2.** Dead on arrival: the shipped kernel already fits the omitted tail as a one-parameter aggregate with a universal depth schedule, which absorbs any global rescaling of the retained part by construction. Norm restoration is a strictly worse special case of a fit we already run.

**One item already shipped before the study.** The Lüders-update / precision-weighted-blend idea from cluster 6 points at real money (MC seed sd 0.031–0.105 at layer 16 against across-network sd 0.049–0.122 — MC noise is the same size as all remaining network-dependent signal), but results.txt §19 already deploys a blend at 0.75. The papers supply no shrinkage estimator, no finite-sample variance analysis, no bias-variance tradeoff — only `N→∞` consistency. The improvement available here is a control-variate coefficient `β = Cov(MC, analytic)/Var(analytic)` computed rather than swept, and that is textbook variance reduction, not operator algebra.

## 4. Negative results worth keeping

Ranked by mechanistic content, not by rhetorical fit.

1. **Abanov et al. 2507.21135, Appendix C — the only mechanistic in-corpus prediction of obstruction 1.** "Deep quantum configurations, characterized by `‖[X_a,X_b]‖₂ = O(‖X_a X_b‖₂)`... This holds e.g. for random matrices... their spectral distribution near the cutoff is very distinct from geometric configurations (power law versus sqrt(x))." Sixteen He-initialised matrices are exactly this class; the claim is that there is no IR/UV separation in the commutator-Laplacian spectrum, so low modes carry no privileged share — a mechanism for the flat-in-rank signature. Same appendix: non-local observables `|x⟩⟨y| + h.c.` sit at high Laplacian energy and are discarded *first* by smoothness-ranked truncation, which is obstruction 5 crossed with obstruction 1. Caveat: it is a one-paragraph aside, no equations, explicitly "out of the scope of this work". Steinacker 1911.03162 (l.163–167) says the same in prose ("most of the configuration space is basically 'white noise'").
2. **Schneiderbauer–Steinacker 1601.08007 §7.1 — an applicability test that would decline to run on us.** "If there is no clear hierarchy of eigenvalues of H, the matrices simply do not contain enough information," hard-coded as `λ_crit`. Our transport spectra (23/85/175 directions for 50/90/99% on `h_8 → z_15`) are a smooth power law with no gap. **Keep with the caveat**: it would be right for the wrong reason. Our failure is cancellation, not spectral resolution, so a gap would not have rescued the compressions; the test is blind to the mechanism that binds.
3. **Karabali–Nair §5 — practitioners who tried hard mode projection and abandoned it.** They reject `P(F) = F₊` ("this product is not associative in general... the lack of associativity limits the utility") in favour of a soft penalty. Independent testimony from another field that hard cuts are the wrong *kind* of compression. Their diagnosed mechanism does not match our signature, so no retrodictive power — and the remedy is unavailable to us, because a smooth full-rank weight costs what the exact computation costs, and cost is the only reason we compress.
4. **Latrémolière math/0310214 — the corpus's toolkit *is* our failure set.** Its only two concrete compression maps are the smearing `α^φ = ∫φ(g)α^g dg` (our failed (a) and its rank-graded relatives (c),(d)) and Prop. 5.1's `P_n`, literally the Cartan conditional expectation onto the diagonal of `M_n` (our failed (b)).
5. **Flora et al. — the greedy plateau.** Greedy moment selection sits almost flat near NPA1 for k ≤ 5, reaching NPA2 only at k ≈ 12 where best-subset reaches it at k = 8; diagnosis is that per-item importance scoring is uninformative under higher-order synergy. Independent reproduction of the **signature** of our site_pow flatness (6.844 / 6.791 / 6.772e-7), by a different mechanism (constrained-optimum cooperation, not cancellation).
6. **Araújo et al. — the honest SDP ceiling.** n = 18 with MOSEK, n = 25 with a first-order solver, after abandoning interior point specifically for scale. Redone in their friendliest setting for us, one eigendecomposition of the 2049-side Gram is ~9e10 FLOPs, so the entire per-network budget buys ~24 iterations at *one* layer against 1e2–1e3 needed, times 16 layers. Confirms obstruction 6 for the whole SDP class.
7. **Connes–van Suijlekom: `prop(C(S¹)^(n)) = 2` independently of n**, plus their own statement that `C*(PAP)` is "a non-informative full matrix algebra". **CORRECTED after reading the source directly (2026-09-11); the original dismissal below was wrong on both counts.** The paper's content is a *duality*: the Toeplitz system `C(S¹)^(n) = P_n C(S¹) P_n`, a truncation on the **state/point side**, has `prop = 2` independently of n — two products of retained elements already generate the full envelope, i.e. the structure is gone after two compositions no matter how fine the truncation. Its dual, the Fejér–Riesz system `C*(Z)^(n)` of finitely-supported Fourier coefficients, a truncation on the **observable/moment side**, has `prop = ∞`: it never degenerates under composition. Both truncate the same circle. The original dismissal claimed (a) "our pipeline never multiplies two truncated observables" — false: the covariance closure is a Hermite power series in the retained correlation matrix to order 8, plus `m2 - Ψ²`, plus source terms quadratic in the transported quantities, so we take products of retained objects at every layer; and (b) that `prop = ∞` means the invariant is vacuous — backwards, since `prop = ∞` is the *non-degenerate* side in this paper. The live distinction: **every truncation we have ever implemented is on the state side**, and the state side is the one that degenerates under composition at a rate independent of the truncation parameter, which is obstruction 1's measured signature.
8. **Wu math/0411475 Def. 3.1 axiom (2): `L_{m+n}(v ⊕ w) = max{L_m(v), L_n(w)}`.** The formal statement that Lipschitz structure aggregates summands by max and never lets them interfere. It is a hypothesis, not a theorem, and it is precisely the property our path sum lacks. One line, and it states the mismatch better than a paragraph.

**Credits to withdraw.** Four things read as predictions of our obstructions and are not, and one gets the sign wrong.

- **Freeman et al.'s abelian no-go must be withdrawn outright.** `M_S f = χ_S f` is a valid positivity-preserving discretization iff `μ|_S` is atomic — and our 1024 neurons under counting measure *are* purely atomic, so the theorem predicts our Cartan corner should be a perfectly good discretization, exactly where we measured 2.4–3.1x failure. The cluster's whole positivity-of-discretization anxiety is an artifact of continuous measure spaces and is **vacuous at finite n**, where every coordinate-subset projection is trivially CP.
- **Connes 1989 hyperfiniteness / Bertozzini object-bijectivity** appear to retrodict the failed Cartan corner (literally a conditional expectation onto a hereditary subalgebra) — but both are obstructions about *which subalgebra structures can exist*, and `groupoid-carrier-and-cartan-truncation.md` lines 174–180 measure that axis to be **flat**: the Cartan-compatible corner does slightly *worse* than the Cartan-breaking spectral truncation at every setting. That is disconfirmation, not corroboration.
- **Devi et al.'s fuzzy-sphere non-saturation** shares our signature (never attained at finite n, biased below) but the mechanism is attainment/compactness — no optimal element exists in the algebra rather than its multiplier completion — where ours is conditioning. Same word, different phenomenon.
- **Giannakis–Montgomery's Leibniz defect** and **Balachandran's abelian-restriction information loss** name obstruction 4's predicate ("the reduced description does not determine the joint") without its mechanism. Balachandran in particular predicts generic resolution loss, which is true of any projection and cannot distinguish our failure from ordinary rank truncation — it contains no coherence functional, and our data is *non-monotone* in rank, which pure resolution loss cannot produce.
- **Voiculescu's Kuroda lemma** `|X^{⊕j}|_Φ = j^{1/p}|X|_Φ` is a genuine sublinear aggregate bound with an exponent — but a direct sum is not a sum. Nothing cancels; singular values are redistributed across orthogonal supports, and MSE is exactly additive on orthogonal pieces.

## 5. What the literature does not cover, and where that work actually lives

**Plainly: not one of the 42 papers contains a conditioning analysis of a signed, nearly-cancelling sum.** There is no coherence functional, no average-sign quantity, no condition number of a summation, no bound whose looseness is controlled by the phase alignment of terms rather than their magnitudes. This is not an oversight — it is forced by axis (C). Every functional in the corpus is unitarily invariant, a sup over a ball, or a positivity condition, and all three are structurally blind to the relative sign between terms. Corollary worth keeping: *the difference between two candidate answers to our problem is a finite-rank operator, so any Dixmier-trace-valued objective has exactly zero gradient with respect to our estimate.*

Note also that our invariant is a classical one under a different name. `Σ‖term‖ / ‖Σ term‖ ≈ 11` is the **condition number of a summation**; coherence 0.09 is the same number as an average sign `⟨s⟩ ≈ 0.09` in a signed sampling problem. The homes for it, named as directions and flagged honestly as **not retrieved or verified in this study**:

1. **Numerical analysis of summation.** Higham, *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*, ch. 4, is where the condition number `Σ|a_i|/|Σa_i|` is defined and where the distinction between forward and backward stability is made. The classical remedy (compensated summation, higher precision) does **not** apply, because our error is truncation, not roundoff. What transfers is the framing: an approximation is safe iff its error is a *relative perturbation of the aggregate*, not of the terms. That is our obstruction 2, in the language of the field that owns it.
2. **The quantum Monte Carlo sign problem, and lattice field theory.** The mature literature on exactly our object — a large signed sum whose average sign is ~0.09. The transferable idea class is not truncation but **making the terms more coherent before truncating**: contour deformation and Lefschetz thimbles (Alexandru, Bedaque, Lamm, Warrington), complex Langevin, constrained-path and fixed-node methods, and the density-of-states / LLR approach (Langfeld–Lucini–Rago), which computes a smooth density first and does the oscillatory integral in one dimension afterwards — structurally what our rank-one kernel does, and the closest published analogue to our one working device. **This is the class the corpus is missing entirely.**
3. **Fast multipole / hierarchical matrices.** Arguably the closest existing technology to our path sum by *shape*: sources on units, a transport kernel, targets, and a sum in which near-cancellation is the norm. FMM error is controlled relative to the potential, not term-by-term, and far-field compression is by analytic expansion rather than mode selection. If any compression of the path sum is going to work, it will look like this and not like `PaP`.
4. **Randomized numerical linear algebra with reweighting.** Leverage-score sampling and sketching give `(1±ε)` *relative* guarantees on `‖Ax‖` because samples are reweighted by `1/p` and are therefore unbiased on the sum — in direct contrast to top-k selection, which is biased by construction. This is the honest home for experiment 3.0, and it explains cleanly why every corpus method fails: binary include/exclude is term-preserving by definition.
5. **Variance reduction.** Control variates on the 2400-row MC channel, with the analytic kernel as the control and a computed rather than swept coefficient. Directly indicated by the measurement that MC seed noise equals all remaining network-dependent signal.

**Two things the study established that no paper is needed for, and that are worth more than the corpus.**

*First, the strategic constraint.* The perfect-marginals-at-every-layer oracle is 7.07e-7, and the leader is at 1.93e-8 raw — **36.6x beyond it**. No improvement to a per-layer marginal closure can reach the frontier. The remaining 45x lives in the joint, in a sampling design, or in exploiting the He ensemble's universality far harder than we do. This is consistent with obstruction 5: the off-diagonal `κ(z_a,z_a,z_b)` — a joint object — is where the money already is.

*Second, the binding constraint on the best known lever is the FLOP meter, not knowledge.* The two-point channel at depth is worth 3.7x and costs ~49% of budget. Under `max(0.1, C/B)`, moving from 9.79% (multiplier at the 0.1 floor) to 49% multiplies the score by 4.9 while dividing raw MSE by 3.7 — a net **1.32x loss**. Break-even is at **37%**. So the question is entirely engineering: *can the deep two-point channel be delivered for under 37% of `2^41` instead of 49%?* A 25% cost reduction on one known channel converts a measured 3.7x into a real gain. The corpus contains no cost model at all and cannot help with this; our own flopscope table can.

## 6. Reading list (five items)

1. **Abanov et al., arXiv:2507.21135, Appendix C — 1 page.** The only mechanistic prediction anywhere in the corpus of our measured flat-in-rank failure: random matrices are "deep quantum", their commutator-Laplacian spectrum is power law rather than sqrt, so there is no IR/UV separation and low modes carry no privileged share.
2. **Rieffel, math/0011063, Section 8 only — ~5 pages.** The corpus's one explicit instance-universal truncation rate. Read the proof to state precisely why our four compressions fail: the theorem's hypothesis is that the truncated grading *is* the grading defining the seminorm, which is exactly the alignment we measured to be absent.
3. **Connes–van Suijlekom, arXiv:2004.14115, §2.3, §4.3, §5 — ~10 pages.** Choi–Effros duality is the one place where "truncate on the point side" vs. "truncate on the moment side" is a theorem rather than a slogan — vocabulary for the aggregate-vs-terms distinction we currently state informally. Already in our bibliography. **Provenance: arXiv:1911.04620 is a different paper entirely** (Zheng et al., Dirichlet Hawkes Process, cs.LG).
4. **Aguilar–Kaad–Kyed, Section 3 only (Prop. 3.9, Thm 3.10) — ~3 pages.** The single provably non-amplifying compression in 42 papers, with its symmetry dependencies fully exposed. Read as the *negative* template: it shows what would have to be true for a compression to be harmless, and makes immediately visible that a fixed He-init weight matrix supplies none of it.
5. **Outside the corpus, and where the next real reading should go: the signed-sum conditioning literature.** Higham ch. 4 for the condition number of a summation (our coherence⁻¹), plus one survey of sign-problem mitigation (contour deformation / Lefschetz thimbles, and the LLR density-of-states method). **Not retrieved or verified in this study** — named as a direction, from background knowledge, because it is the only body of work that treats the obstruction that actually binds us. If one item is read next, make it this one and not another spectral triple.
