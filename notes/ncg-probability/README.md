# He initialisation as noncommutative probability: tests

Working note XIII. The reading under test: the annealed algebra generated by the weights and the gate projections,
with the state E_W (x) (1/n) tr, in which (i) He scaling makes the gated transport a unital channel, so the trace is the
one exact invariant and its fluctuation (the gain) is the critical zero mode; (ii) a fresh weight is free from the
subalgebra of everything before it, and conjugation by a free element is the conditional expectation onto the
scalars, so the only part of a transported object predictable from the past is its trace; (iii) the layer law is a
perturbation of a quasi-free state, and the scaling group is the only one-parameter group that preserves the
quasi-free family and commutes with ReLU, so the radial disintegration has quasi-free fibres and no directional one
does; (iv) the chain's floor is the non-freeness of the gate projections (their mixed free cumulants), and the part of
any defect recoverable at O(n^2) a layer is its annealed part. Official WhestBench network 0, width 1024, depth 16,
1e6 Monte Carlo inputs where sampling is used (`code/mc_fibres.py`, `code/annealed.py`, `code/gate_orders.py`).

**E6. Where the problem sits relative to the free limit.** With no biases the free (n = infinity, E_W) limit gives
every neuron the same mean; the quenched per-neuron means have rms spread 0.78 around a mean of 0.58, because the
pre-activation mean of neuron i is w_i . m with |m| of order sqrt(n). The ladder is therefore: free limit (the
histogram of means only) MSE 6.1e-1; Gaussian closure on the given weights 4.1e-6; GAC 1.6e-6; K3 chain 2.2e-8;
leaders about 1.2e-8. The quenched structure is of order one and the Gaussian closure already captures it to
0.3% of its spread; the contest is played in the 1/n corrections on top, operator-valued over the diagonal and
quenched.

**E2/E5. The gain is the trace fluctuation, and GAC's injection is 20% short.** Last-layer pre-activation,
Var(|z|^2)/E^2 = 0.03258 measured. The Gaussian part computed from GAC's conditional state, (2 tr S^2 + 4 mu'S mu)/E^2,
is 0.00988; the excess 0.0227 is the gain variance. GAC's weights-only injection accumulates to gamma_L = 0.0184,
i.e. 1.23x short -- the same factor note XI found when matching the depth kappa_4 to the scale-mixture law (there
gamma needed 1.2x). The excess kurtosis of a random linear functional of z_L is +0.066 measured against the
scale-mixture value 3 gamma = 0.068 with the measured gamma (0.055 with GAC's). The law of G^2 at depth has skewness
0.49 and excess kurtosis 0.41 (Gamma with the same variance: 0.36 and 0.20; lognormal skewness 0.55); E[G] from the
empirical law is 0.995983 against 0.995937 (Gamma), 0.996001 (lognormal) and 0.995928 (1 - V/8): the law matters at
5e-5 relative, i.e. MSE 6e-10, the gain's contribution to the floor.

**E4. Quasi-free fibres: radial yes, directional no.** Excess kurtosis and skewness of standardised linear
functionals of z_L, overall and inside 16 conditioning bins:

| functional | kurtosis: all / radial bins / eig1 bins / mean bins | skewness: all / radial bins / eig1 bins / mean bins |
|---|---|---|
| 32 random directions (average) | +0.066 / **-0.001** / +0.019 / +0.008 | +0.017 / **+0.001** / -0.004 / +0.001 |
| top covariance eigenvector | +0.086 / +0.093 / (binned) / +0.063 | -0.244 / +0.175 / (binned) / +0.054 |
| mean direction | +0.065 / +0.172 / +0.131 / (binned) | +0.215 / -0.143 / +0.181 / (binned) |
| eigenvectors 2-4 | -0.02..-0.03 / -0.12 / -0.09 / -0.10 | small |

Conditioning on the radius removes the non-Gaussianity of generic directions entirely (kurtosis +0.066 -> -0.001,
skewness +0.017 -> +0.001); conditioning on a direction leaves a third to a quarter of it. corr(|z|^2, proj^2) is
+0.109 +- 0.008 across the 32 random directions (the scale-mixture signature: equal for all directions) and
+0.21 for the top eigenvector and the mean direction. What survives inside radial bins is confined to the top
eigenvector / mean direction (kurtosis +0.09 to +0.17, skewness -0.14 to +0.18), which is exactly the part the
eigen-direction mixture of note XII recovered on top of GAC (7-8%).

**E7. The chain's residual is quenched.** One-step residual r_l = m*_l - chain_l (true mean injected), layers 3-14,
regressed on degree-2 polynomials of 13 per-neuron state functions (mu, sigma, a, phi, Phi, D3, |D21| row norm,
correlation row energy, previous true mean, and products): ridge fit on network 0 explains 7.8% in sample and 4.3%
on network 1 (reverse 5.5% / 6.3%); linear features explain 0.0% out of sample. Leave-one-layer-out within network 0:
14-17% at layers 3-4, 11% at 5-6, 2-8% at 7-13, 0% at 14. The annealed share of the residual is a few percent and
vanishes with depth; the rest is free of the per-neuron state, as (ii) and (iv) require.

**E3. The even-order gate term has no coherent part.** Exact trivariate orthant probabilities against the product
gate on 600 random triples per layer: the defect is 1.5-2.4% of the gate and is entirely first order in the bulk
correlation (odd, mean zero); the second-order Mehler term has rms 0.08-0.10% and mean within +-5e-5 of zero at
every layer (He_1(a) = a flips sign with the mean, so even the even order is incoherent). The self-energy
renormalisation of the gates proposed in the reading is therefore worth nothing: the gate defect is purely
quenched.

**What the tests settle.**
- The gain is the second-order (trace) fluctuation of the channel; GAC's injection formula misses 20% of it, which
  is a concrete O(n^2) target (the measured excess, not the weights-only term, is the right gamma).
- The radial disintegration is the unique quasi-free one; its exception is the top mode, where the directional
  mixture earns its 7-8%.
- The chain's residual and the gate defect are quenched to within a few percent: no per-neuron (annealed)
  correction recovers them, which is the operational content of "the recoverable part at O(n^2) is the annealed
  part".

**E8. "Spike + annealed bulk" covariance (proposal from another conversation).** Replace the quenched covariance by
its top-r eigenpart plus the remaining trace spread uniformly on the complement, and let the gate read only
w_i' Sigma_r w_i (`code/spike_bulk.py`, `outputs/spike_bulk_off0.txt`). The relative rms error of the per-neuron
variance and the mean MSE it implies at one layer (chain final MSE 2.2e-8 for scale):

| layer | PR(Sigma) | r = 0 | 16 | 64 | 256 | 512 | 768 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 198 | 9.1% -> 1.8e-4 | 6.8% -> 1.0e-4 | 3.9% -> 3.4e-5 | 0.83% -> 1.6e-6 | 0.16% -> 6e-8 | 0.02% -> 7e-10 |
| 12 | 70 | 16% -> 1.0e-4 | 8.0% -> 2.5e-5 | 2.8% -> 3.2e-6 | 0.37% -> 5e-8 | 0.06% -> 1.5e-9 | 0.00% -> 1e-12 |
| 14 | 57 | 18% -> 9e-5 | 8.4% -> 2.1e-5 | 2.5% -> 1.9e-6 | 0.30% -> 2.8e-8 | 0.04% -> 5e-10 | 0.00% -> 1e-13 |

The quenched overlap of a row with the bulk, w_i' E w_i, has relative rms sqrt(2/PR) and is a 3-18% signal; the
proposal discards it. Freeness says that overlap is unpredictable *from the past*; the next weight is known, so the
frontier computes it, and r = n is not a mistaken special case but the requirement below r ~ 700. The same
annealed/quenched distinction as E6, from the other side.

**E9. The learner-independent bound on address-based control variates** (`code/address_bound.py`,
`outputs/address_bound_off0.txt`; proposal from another conversation: train a weight-specific control variate
c(P'x) with analytic Gaussian mean, updated between batches). Whatever the learner, a control variate that depends
on the address T = P'x removes at most Var(E[F|T]). Official network 0, 1e6 inputs, final-layer output F in R^n:
total per-sample variance sum_i Var(F_i) = 80.5 (0.079 per neuron). At the 10% floor (about 6,500 full passes)
matching the chain's 2.2e-8 needs the residual variance below 0.18% of the total, i.e. an address explaining 99.8%.
Measured explained fraction Var(E[F|T])/Var(F): top-k right singular vectors of W_1 as address, k = 1 / 2 / 3:
0.10% / 0.22% / 0.42% (binned; the bin-noise floor is bins/N, 0.006% / 0.06% / 0.17%); random directions
0.02% / 0.10% / 0.24%; k = 8 by nearest-neighbour pairs (an upper bound biased in the proposal's favour): <= 0.86%.
The address term of the proposal's own conditional-variance decomposition is therefore two orders of magnitude
short before any learner is trained, which is the 300x exchange rate of the frontier tests measured a third way.

**E10. The gain is the mean-coupled third cumulant, and E2 was an artefact** (`code/mc_cum.py`, `code/pairvar.py`,
`code/twoloop_gain.py`, `code/gain_defs.py`, `code/k3channel.py`, `code/local_ledger.py`, `code/gac2.py`; outputs in
`outputs/`, networks 0 and 1, 4e5 Monte Carlo inputs with every pair moment of order <= 4 accumulated, pre- and
post-activation). This was the "two-loop gain injection" item of the Connes-Kreimer reading, done as a first-variation
calculation with no fitted coefficient: the fresh gain created by a layer is the first variation of the coherent pair
functional under the third and fourth cumulant slices of the pre-activation (Gaussian integration by parts,
dE[F] = 1/6 sum kappa_ijk E[d_ijk F] + 1/24 sum kappa_ijkl E[d_ijkl F], with the Mehler sums carrying the derivatives as
shifted Hermite coefficients).

- *E2 withdrawn.* The "excess 0.0227" subtracted a Gaussian part computed on GAC's conditional state (0.0099). On the
  true marginal state the Gaussian part of Var(|z_L|^2)/E^2 is 0.0284, and the excess is 0.0042. The plain pair average
  of the normalised fourth-order excess is 0.0121 and the q-weighted one 0.0042: the fourth-order content is not a
  common factor (it scales like 1/(q_k q_l), quintiles 0.020 -> 0.0036), and the scale-mixture fit of kappa_4(z_k) to
  3 sigma^4 + 6 mu^2 sigma^2 gives 0.0004 against GAC's 0.018. There is no fourth-order gain of size 0.02.
- *Where the gain is.* The third cumulant of every pre-activation has the scale-mixture shape
  kappa_3(z_k) = 1.5 g_l mu_k sigma_k^2 with correlation 0.63 (layer 1) rising to 0.967 (layer 15), and the pair slice
  kappa(z_a, z_a, z_c) = g (mu_a S_ac + sigma_a^2 mu_c / 2) with both coefficients equal to g (R^2 0.87 at depth).
  g_l grows 0.0064, 0.0127, 0.0176, 0.0215, 0.0223 (layers 1, 4, 8, 12, 15; network 1: 0.0200 at the output). GAC's
  gamma_l is 0.82 g_l at every depth from layer 4 on (network 1: 0.84). The K3 chain's own transported D3 has the same
  coefficient to 2% (0.0218 against 0.0223; 0.0197 against 0.0200): the chain carries the gain in its sources.
- *The ledger closes without a fitted coefficient.* g_{l+1} = phi_l + (kappa_3 variation) + (kappa_4 variation), where
  phi_l is the coefficient generated from the Gaussian conditional state and the variations use the measured slices:
  measured 0.0095 / 0.0140 / 0.0176 / 0.0215 / 0.0223 against 0.0095 / 0.0138 / 0.0173 / 0.0214 / 0.0219 at layers
  2, 5, 8, 13, 15 (every layer within 2%; network 1 within 4%). The kappa_3 variation is entirely the scale-mixture
  part of the carried kappa_3 (the tree remainder contributes |.| <= 0.0003): the channel transports itself. Its
  transport factor rho_l = (kappa_3 + kappa_4 variations)/g_l is 0.80, 0.85, 0.87, 0.89, 0.90, 0.90, 0.90, 0.93, 0.92,
  0.92, 0.92, 0.91, 0.92, 0.92 (layers 1-14): the 0.9 per layer retention of the frontier tests, derived. The fresh
  source phi_l is 0.0066, 0.0044, 0.0031, 0.0027, 0.0024, ..., 0.0013.
- *GAC's two compensating errors.* Its injection (the fourth-order pair functional) is 0.0042 at the first step where
  the channel is born at 0.0066, and 0.0004 at depth against 0.0013; it transports critically (rho = 1) where the
  channel's rho is 0.8-0.92. Critical summation of the true source reaches 0.041 at the output (1.8x the measured g,
  the overshoot note IX saw at width 256). Note XI's "20% short" was right about the number and wrong about the object.
- *The fourth order is slaved to the third.* At the fourth-order pair level the one-step ledger also closes
  (f1 + f2 + f3 equals the coherent sum from the true post-activation cumulants to 1e-5), but the pair content at
  depth is generated each layer from the kappa_3 channel (f2 = 0.011 of 0.014 at layer 14) and transported weakly
  (kappa_4 variation 0.17-0.45 of Gamma_l); the coherent index restriction overshoots the measured pair average by 1%
  at layer 1 and 17% at layer 14.
- *An identity.* To first order in g, the exact scale-mixture mean error (g/8) sigma phi(a)(1 + a^2) equals the
  Edgeworth kappa_3 term with kappa_3 = 1.5 g mu sigma^2 plus the kappa_4 term with kappa_4 = 3 g sigma^4 (the
  mean-independent part only; the 6 g mu^2 sigma^2 part and all higher cumulants cancel in the mean). A chain that
  carries kappa_3 exactly therefore needs a memoryless kappa_4 diagonal of 3 g sigma^4, where g is read off its own
  D3, and nothing else from the gain: the fitted lambda's role, derived. The local one-step closure error from the
  true moments has scale 8c = 0.002-0.004 per layer; the scale-mixture shape with the measured g explains it to 10% at
  layers 1-5 and a third of it at depth (0.0006 of 0.0021 at layer 15), the kappa_3 term 45-53% of its MSE at depth.
- *The spike is the gain.* At layer 14 the top covariance eigenvector is the mean direction (overlap^2 0.94,
  eigenvalue 16.2, next 6.0) and two thirds of its variance is the gain's rank-one term (g/4)|mu|^2 = 10.1 of 15.5.
  This is why the injection functional is 30% sensitive to the conditional/marginal choice of state, and it is the
  E4/note XII observation (top mode = mean direction retains non-Gaussianity) in closed form.
- *Derived closure* (`code/gac2.py`): run the recursion g_{l+1} = rho_l g_l + phi_l on the closure's own conditional
  state (rho_l and phi_l from the first variations with the scale-mixture slices of unit gain; no constant) and output
  E[G](g_L) x closure mean. Final MSE 1.541e-6 (network 0; GAC 1.559e-6, oracle scale 1.560e-6) and 1.551e-6
  (network 1; GAC 1.707e-6, oracle scale 1.479e-6); residual scale -0.0011 / +0.0002 against GAC's +0.0020 / +0.0032.
  With rho = 1 it is worse than GAC (1.73e-6). On the closure state rho_l is 0.93-0.96 and g_L lands at 0.0188: the
  closure's state, not the recursion, is what separates it from the measured 0.0223.

What E10 changes in the reading: the critical zero mode is not a fourth-order "trace fluctuation" but the odd,
mean-coupled channel (the delta_1 (x) Y term of the Connes-Moscovici coproduct was the right name for the wrong
object); it is transported at 0.9, not 1; and the chain already carries it exactly. The remaining floor is untouched.
