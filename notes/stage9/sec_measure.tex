The decisive line is the pair in the middle: the organisers' exact first-order chain, ported to numpy and matched
to the torch reference bit for bit, reaches $5.6\cdot10^{-7}$ on network $0$ when its fourth cumulant is dropped,
the accuracy of our own chain, and $3.5\cdot10^{-8}$ when the single radial scalar
$c=\tfrac{3}{n(n+2)}(\sum_{a\ne b}\kap_4(h_a,h_a,h_b,h_b)+\sum_a\kap_4(h_a))$ per layer is kept. One number per layer,
the collective scale of Lemma~\ref{lem:scale}, is worth a factor $16$ in MSE; our chain's transported $(2,2)$ slice,
which carries $n^2$ numbers and is harmful off the diagonal, was worth nothing. The reference's own naive count is
$3.4\cdot10^{12}$ FLOPs ($1.5B$ in float32), $93\%$ of it in the transport and readout of the factored $\kap_3$ whose rank
grows by $2n$ per layer; the cost problem is the memory of the third cumulant, not the fourth.

On network $1$ the same measurement gives correlation $0.951$, $a=0.500$, $93\%$ explained and
$4.72\cdot10^{-6}\to3.45\cdot10^{-7}$; the fitted coefficients cross over ($a_0$ on network $1$: $3.68\cdot10^{-7}$;
$a_1$ on network $0$: $4.51\cdot10^{-7}$), and the parameter-free midpoint $(e+\Delta e)/2$ gives $4.50$ and
$3.45\cdot10^{-7}$: the weight of Corollary~\ref{cor:eldan} integrates to one half on these networks, so the
\emph{Euler--Stein midpoint} is an estimator with no fitted constant that is nine to fourteen times better than the
chain it corrects.

For the exact first-order chain the same measurement ($2049$ runs of \texttt{kprop3} on Modal, $41$ s each on four
cores) gives $|\delta(0)|_{\rm rms}=6.6\cdot10^{-4}$ against an error of $1.9\cdot10^{-4}$, correlation $0.902$, $81\%$ of
the mean square explained, $a=0.255$, and a corrected MSE of $6.64\cdot10^{-9}$: a factor $5.3$ below the chain and
a factor two below the leaders' raw accuracy, from a correction that is estimator-agnostic and derived, not fitted,
except for the one constant $a$. The midpoint $a=\tfrac12$ does not apply to this chain ($3.3\cdot10^{-8}$): its
defect is second order in the births, so it decays along the path like the square of the kink density,
$(1+\tau)^{-1}$, which integrates against the weight to $\tfrac13$, and the measured $0.255$ sits below that by the
same direction rotation seen on the small network. The cost of this correction is $2n+1$ chain evaluations, which
is why the symbolic second-order response of Theorem~\ref{thm:gram} is the open engineering problem of this note.
The constant is a property of the chain, not of the network: on network $1$ the same $2049$ runs give
correlation $0.914$, $84\%$ explained, $a=0.257$ and $4.38\cdot10^{-8}\to7.15\cdot10^{-9}$, and the two constants
cross over without loss ($a_0$ on network $1$: $7.152\cdot10^{-9}$; $a_1$ on network $0$: $6.646\cdot10^{-9}$).
A single constant $a\approx0.256$, fixed offline, corrects the exact first-order chain by a factor $5$--$6$ on
every network tried.

Two readings. First, the Euler--Stein defect of the \emph{Gaussian} closure, with no cumulant machinery at all,
reaches the accuracy of our third-cumulant chain: on network $0$ the exact $\delta(0)$ (coordinate Laplacian,
$2049$ chain evaluations on Modal) has correlation $0.931$ with the error, explains $89\%$ of its mean square,
and the fitted $a=0.535$ is close to the parameter-free midpoint $a=\tfrac12$. Theorem~\ref{thm:defect} is
exact; the $11\%$ not explained is the variation of $\delta(y)$ over the Eldan path. Second, the organisers'
exact first-order $K=3$ reference is $13\times$ better than our chain and carries no fitted constant, so the
gap is in the first-order transport, not in calibration or in $\kap_4$.

The old content is not a scaled copy of the young content: regressing the $(2,1)$-slice contribution of the
sources of age $\ge2$ on that of age $1$ (\texttt{scripts/diag\_oldshape.py}) gives correlations $0.29$--$0.43$ and
a residual of $0.90$--$0.96$ of its norm at every layer (diagonal $\kap_3$: correlations $0.43$--$0.61$), while the old
content grows from $0.33$ to $3.1$ times the young content between layers $3$ and $15$. Memoryless regeneration
of $\kap_3$ from the current layer with per-layer scalars would keep a tenth of it.

The old content splits into a coherent and an incoherent part. Replacing the old sources' $(2,1)$ slices by their
best rank-one approximation recovers most of what dropping them loses ($3.7\cdot10^{-6}\to2.2\cdot10^{-7}$, against
$3.5\cdot10^{-8}$ for the full memory), and the remainder converges slowly in rank (rank $64$: $7.8\cdot10^{-8}$). This
is what the Hadamard structure predicts: for products of gated random matrices the Hadamard squares split into
a separable mean (rank one per term, computable from the variance profile of the cocycle and its action on a
few vectors at $O(n^2)$) and incoherent fluctuations, which decay with age but are still large at ages two to five.

\paragraph{Collective mode and local features (four networks).} The top eigenvector of the pre-activation covariance
carries $0.9\%$ of its Frobenius energy at layer $0$ and $37$--$48\%$ at layer $15$ (top $64$: $35\%\to96\%$), and
aligns with the mean to $\cos=0.97$--$0.98$ from layer $12$ on. Sources born at layer $4$ or later keep their collective
direction under transport ($\cos(T_{l\leftarrow l'}p_{l'},m_l)=0.78$--$0.98$, improving with $l'$); sources born at
layers $0$--$3$ have no collective direction to keep ($\cos$ between $-0.3$ and $0.6$), and the family
$\{T_{l\leftarrow l'}p_{l'}\}_{l'}$ at $l=15$ has normalised singular values $(1,0.32,0.29,0.23)$: one dominant
direction and a tail from the early births. The per-neuron error of the exact chain is not predictable from the
chain's own local features (\texttt{scripts/diag\_features.py}: $34$ features, ridge regression trained on one
network makes the other worse, $1.0\cdot10^{-6}$ against $4.4\cdot10^{-8}$ raw; in-sample it removes $5\%$), and its
collective share is $0.2$--$5\%$; the error of the window-one chain is $53$--$64\%$ collective. Dropping the memory
shifts the mean collectively; the exact chain's residual is incoherent and local features do not see it.

\paragraph{The four parts, measured (four networks).} Reading the old sources (age $\ge w$) through the parts of
Proposition~\ref{prop:parts}, dense window $w$, network $0$ [range over networks $0$--$3$]: coherent part only,
$w=2$: $1.6$ [$1.6$--$2.1$]$\cdot10^{-6}$, $w=4$: $7.3$ [$7.1$--$9.3$]$\cdot10^{-7}$, no better than dropping the old
sources ($1.65\cdot10^{-6}$, $7.5\cdot10^{-7}$); coherent $+$ cross: $1.6$ / $7.3\cdot10^{-7}$, nothing; coherent $+$ diagonal
memory: $1.2\cdot10^{-6}$ / $5.4\cdot10^{-7}$; all three: $1.2\cdot10^{-6}$ / $5.3\cdot10^{-7}$; all three $+$ the star's
bulk: $7.3$ / $3.2\cdot10^{-7}$; all three $+$ the residual's bulk: $3.1$ / $1.3\cdot10^{-7}$; everything (dense):
$3.5\cdot10^{-8}$. The memory is the bulk: the hub-diagonal pairing of the incoherent parts of the birth
covariance and of the residual, which no collective quantity sees. The coherent and cross parts, the only ones
that accumulate across sources at $O(n^2)$ or $O(n^3)$ for the whole memory, carry less than a third of a
decade. This is the measured content of law (L1) of Section~\ref{sec:reading}: the transverse measure of the
hub foliation is uniform, and the memory is its full support. Any representation of the old sources that keeps
them to the chain's precision must keep the bulk pairing, whose cost is the crossover law.

The birth blocks are not sparsifiable in the hub index either. Keeping, in every star and residual block, only
the $qn$ columns of largest importance ($w_{2,j}|a_j|^2|c_j|^2$ for the star, the column norm for the residual
leg; \texttt{srcfrac} in \texttt{whest/kprop3c.py}) costs a factor $12$ at $q=\tfrac12$ and $43$ at
$q=\tfrac14$, and a least-squares rescaling of the kept columns changes nothing (the dropped and kept columns
are orthogonal in the slice inner product, so the rescaling factor is one). The importance profile is flat:
at He initialisation every hub neuron contributes a comparable, mutually orthogonal rank-one term, which is the
regime in which subset selection cannot work (Section~\ref{sec:reading}).

Three conclusions. (i) The old sources are essential and their effect is smooth in amplitude but not in shape: a
$10\%$ scale error on everything older than two layers costs a factor $1.5$, dropping them costs $100$, and a
Frobenius truncation that keeps $70$--$87\%$ of their energy ($k=128$) costs $26$, exactly the Hadamard
obstruction of Section~\ref{sec:cost}. (ii) A window of four dense ages with a $k=384$ shared basis for the rest is
lossless to $5\%$ (two ages: a factor $2.2$; four ages with $k=256$: $1.5$), reproducing the open-source chain's regime, and costs about $1B$ in float32 without the
identity-leg and Strassen savings ($\approx0.45B$ with them); the floor is $0.1B$. (iii) The radial scalar is
exactly right as projected and the chain is sharply sensitive to it ($\pm10\%$: $2.5\times$ and $8\times$), so the
central variable is not where calibration helps; float32 reproduces float64 to all digits shown.

\subsection{Where the chain's error is injected}
With $e_l=\mathrm{out}_l-u_l$ and the injected part $e_l-\Phi_l\!\cdot\!(W_le_{l-1})$ (the error not explained by
the linear propagation of the previous layer's error through the gates), \texttt{scripts/injected.py} gives rms
injected errors of $2.0,1.1,1.6,2.1,2.3,2.5,2.9,2.7,2.8,2.9,2.8,2.7,2.8,2.9,3.0\cdot10^{-4}$ at layers $1$--$15$
against a total rms error growing from $2.0\cdot10^{-4}$ to $7.6\cdot10^{-4}$: the chain injects a nearly constant
$3\cdot10^{-4}$ per layer from layer $4$ on, and the injected error has no coherent component along the mean
direction (share $<10^{-2}$ beyond layer $2$). This is the signature of a \emph{per-neuron}, incoherent deficit
in the transported slices, consistent with Section~\ref{sec:central}: the transported $D21$ has correlation
$0.965$ with Monte Carlo, i.e.\ an incoherent relative error of $\sqrt{1-0.965^2}\approx26\%$ on top of the
$8$--$10\%$ coherent deficit, and by the empirical law of \cite{Wu}'s reimplementation (extra MSE
$\approx4\cdot10^{-6}\varepsilon^2$ for a relative rms slice error $\varepsilon$) a slice error of $35\%$ accounts for
the whole $5.7\cdot10^{-7}$.

\subsection{What the open-source chain does that ours does not}
A reading of the open-source chain's derivation notes against \texttt{k3chain3.py} (delegated, cross-checked on
the formulas) locates the difference in exactly the first-order transport: (i) its slice update carries every
elementwise diagram of first order in the cumulants, i.e.\ the $\rho$-dependence of the gates
($D21_{ac}\,C_{ac}^k\times$Wick coefficients) and the $\kap_3(z_a)$-driven terms, where ours keeps the gates at
$\rho=0$; each such term is $O(\rho)\approx3\%$ per layer and compounds over fifteen transports; (ii) its fourth
cumulant is the exact diagonal plus an isotropic off-diagonal $\lambda_lC^{\rm off}$ with one fitted $\lambda_l$ per
layer, and it \emph{feeds} $\kap_4$ into the fully off-diagonal $\kap_3$ (the largest single term beyond the base
chain, $2\times$ in MSE), whereas our transported $(2,2)$ slice is measured by them to be harmful off the
diagonal ($1.47\times$ worse than isotropic); (iii) the feedback coefficients of the GC arm are
$(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$ by the first-order Edgeworth count against our $(1,\tfrac12)$; on network $0$ changing
ours to $\tfrac12$ moves the MSE from $5.74$ to $6.04\cdot10^{-7}$, so this is not the bottleneck. The
$\rho$-exact gates of (i) are the closed forms of Section~\ref{sec:births}: by Lemma~\ref{lem:shift} the
coefficient of $D21_{ac}$ in $\kap_3(h_a,h_a,h_c)$ is $G_{ac}-\mu_aw_a\Phi\big((\alpha_c-\rho_{ac}\alpha_a)/\sqrt{1-\rho_{ac}^2}\big)$
with $G_{ac}=\E[\theta_a\theta_c]$ the gate covariance \eqref{eq:gate}, which reduces to $t_a\Phi_c$ at $\rho=0$,
and likewise for the transposed slice and the $(2,2)$ feed with conditional means and densities at $z_c=0$.
Every coefficient of the first-order transport is a Hermite series obtained by index shifts, which is how a
system built on this note generates its term table (Section~\ref{sec:dictionary}).
