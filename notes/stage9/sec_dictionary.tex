\subsection{What the measurements prescribe for a system, and what is open}
The decisive constraints are the pair floor of Proposition~\ref{prop:floor} ($12.8\,n^3$ per layer at the $0.1B$ floor),
the price of one source-age of exact first-order memory ($12\,n^3$, Section~\ref{sec:cost}), and the error
accounting of Section~\ref{sec:measure}: the central variable is one scalar per layer and worth a factor $16$; the
memory of $\kap_3$ is worth a factor $100$, is smooth in amplitude and brittle in shape; a window of four ages with a
$k=384$ tier is lossless and costs about $0.5B$ with every structural saving; the leaders sit at $0.1B$ with three
times the accuracy of the exact first-order chain. In this order of expected value:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Memory, not regeneration.} The shape diagnostic of Section~\ref{sec:measure} rules out the naive
version of the following, so it is stated as the open problem it is:  The old sources' effect is smooth in amplitude ($10\%$
scale: factor $1.5$) and their transport is the collective mode; the open-source chain already regenerates the
off-diagonal $\kap_4$ from the covariance with one fitted $\lambda_l$ per layer. The analogous move for $\kap_3$ would be
to keep one or two dense ages and replace the rest by a function of the current state (the gated covariance and
the current slices) with per-layer fitted scalars, fitted on the public networks against the dense chain's slices
rather than against the final means. The theory says what the function should be: the old content is the image
of births under the cocycle, i.e.\ Hadamard products of transported covariances, so the ansatz is the star block
of the present layer with its own gates scaled by the cocycle's decay.
\item \textbf{Euler--Stein merge.} $e-a\,\delta(0)$ with $a$ fitted once: a factor $9$ for the Gaussian closure at
$n=1024$ ($a=0.54$, midpoint $a=\tfrac12$ nearly as good), measured for the exact first-order chain in
Section~\ref{sec:measure}. Affordable only through the closable part of the second-order response of
Theorem~\ref{thm:gram}, whose five objects are the same as a $\kap_3$ chain's; whether it correlates with the exact
$\delta$ as well as $\delta$ with the error is the open experiment.
\item \textbf{Identity-leg structure and Strassen} for whatever dense memory is kept: $12\,n^3$ per source-age, $8.3$
with Strassen, against the $24$ of the reference code.
\item \textbf{Noncommutative version.} The gates $\theta(z_a)$ of one layer generate a commutative algebra; across
layers the gate algebras do not commute with the weights, and the chain's closure is a product state on the gate
algebra of layer $l$ conditioned on the surrogate of layer $l-1$. The stage-8 KMS condition for the modular flow of
the Gaussian state, restricted to the gate algebra, is the heat equation of Section~\ref{sec:births}; its failure
across layers is the defect. Writing the chain as a state on the free product of the gate algebras, with the
trickle-down of \cite{AKV} as its Cartan expansion, is the theoretical continuation; the cocycle of
Section~\ref{sec:cost} is its transverse measure.
\end{enumerate}
