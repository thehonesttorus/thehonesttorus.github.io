# Where the error is generated: gate facets acting on the carried sources

Working note XXXIII. A theory synthesis, written after notes XXXI and XXXII, that reads the measured facts through the
cumulant-response theory (the missing third-to-fourth feed, its Gaussian score and facet representation, the
homogeneity Ward identity, the conditional factor construction, and the closure, compression and memory theorems)
and through the K4 reset (THEORY_3). No experiment was run for it. It revises the strategic reading given before the
Azure work, which said that the fourth-order sector was "closed in closed form", that closure refinement was worth a
few percent, and that a factor of three could not come from accuracy inside the moment-chain class. The first two
were statements in the wrong norm and the third is contradicted by the oracle ceiling.

## 0. The facts the theory has to account for

All from notes XXXI and XXXII (Monte Carlo of 1.6e7 inputs, noise-free extrapolation; network 1 complete, network 0
replicated for the combined oracles).

- **F1, the ceiling.** When every pre-activation statistic that the chain's local maps read (the third-cumulant
  readouts D3 and D21, the fourth-cumulant diagonal and its (2,2) and (3,1) slices) is replaced by truth at every
  layer, the error falls by 91% (network 1, raw 1.8e-9) and 98% (network 0, at the reference's own noise floor). The
  mean, variance and covariance stay the chain's own.
- **F2, regeneration.** Fed true inputs at every layer, the chain's next-layer fourth-cumulant slices are as wrong as
  in the free-running chain (diagonal 14-33%, (2,2) 16-32%, (3,1) 74-90%). The defect is made afresh by each layer's
  map; almost none is inherited.
- **F3, coherence.** One true slice alone makes the output worse ((2,2): +13%); the whole sector true is worth -33%,
  twice the diagonal alone; on top of true third-cumulant readouts it takes -56% to -91% (network 1) and -78% to -98%
  (network 0).
- **F4, the split.** The third-cumulant readouts carry about 40% of the error; the fourth-cumulant sector carries most
  of the rest once they are right.
- **F5, downstream.** True variance alone is worth 37-52%, but nothing once the readouts and the sector are true: the
  variance's 0.1% errors are produced by them, through the pair program.

## 1. The closure theorem reads the ceiling

The theory separates a state map's defect into coefficient error and missing information:

    R_l = U_l J_(l+1) - J_l M_l^*,   R_l^* R_l = (C_l - M_l^*)^*(C_l - M_l^*) + E_l^* E_l,   E_l = Q_l U_l J_(l+1),

with E_l = 0 exactly when every pulled-back future query lies in the retained space.

**F1 says the local queries are answered by the retained state.** At each layer the mean map and the pair program read
only pair-restricted cumulants up to order four, and with those exact the terminal error is at the noise floor. In the
identifiability language of the synthesis: the residual r_F of each local query after projection on the retained
features is small, and the bivariate Edgeworth law that the pair program uses is an admissible closure for them. Law
closure of the local maps is at most a few percent (note XXXII section 5).

**F1 and F2 together say the defect is in the state map, and the theory says which part.** The pair-restricted
fourth-cumulant state is not closed: the next layer's pair slices are contractions of the whole post-activation
fourth cumulant, including its (3,1), (2,1,1) and (1,1,1,1) classes, which the pair state does not hold. But section 2
shows that, to first order, those classes are generated by objects the chain does carry: the third-cumulant sources (as
full tensors, in the legs), the covariance and the scale mode. Relative to the retained space extended by those
generators, the fourth-cumulant defect is coefficient error, C_l - M_l^*, not missing information. F2 says that errors in
the carried pair state hardly propagate: the defect is made at each step from the omitted classes, not inherited
through the retained ones. It does not measure the memory of the omitted classes themselves, since the oracle never
supplies them (note XXXIV section 3). That memory is not short: the scale mode carries triple-class content
(g var_a C_bc) with eigenvalue one by the Ward identity. But it is one exactly transported scalar, not a new tensor, so
the extended state needs the scale gain carried explicitly and no other new dynamical object at first order. That
the rest of the omitted memory is small is a hypothesis, measured only in the gain projection (note XXI).

**What this overturns.** The class's ceiling with its retained statistics computed correctly is roughly ten times
below the present error and five to eight times below the leaders' raw error (1.1-1.5e-8). The binding constraint is
not the representation class but the cost of computing the retained statistics correctly.

## 2. Where the fourth cumulant sits in the response theory

To first order in the non-Gaussian tangent of the pre-activation law around its Gaussian reference N(mu, C), the
post-activation fourth cumulant has three origins:

    kappa4(y) = G4(mu, C) + L3[kappa3(z)] + L4[kappa4(z)] + (second order).

- **G4, the one-loop generation.** ReLU of a Gaussian. Its pair classes are what the pair program computes; its omitted
  classes are near zero in the gain projection (note XXI, -0.0001 to -0.0007 in g4 units).
- **L3, the feed from the third-cumulant sources.** Exactly the response
  Delta_T = E[h_T Psi_miss] = <S_T, Pi_3 Psi_miss>: the third-chaos component that ReLU creates in the fourth-order
  influence, paired with the incoming third-order score. It contains bulk terms and single gate facets, never products
  of two facet deltas. Its class structure is graded: a pair source Gamma = D21 feeds the (3,1) class at order one
  (closed form at an independent reference, (3tc/2) ab [a^2 (1+4c^2) + b^2 (1-4c^2)] for row (a, b)); the (2,1,1)
  class enters at second order and the (1,1,1,1) class at fourth order in the strength of the common factor; a
  diagonal source D3 feeds no mixed class at all. In the conditional form, the omitted classes are outer cumulants of the
  conditional covariance response
      d11^(ij)(X) = 1/2 [Gamma_ij m_i''(X) m_j'(X) + Gamma_ji m_i'(X) m_j''(X)],   m' = Phi, m'' = phi / s,
  with the conditional means.
- **L4 along the scale direction is exact.** The mean-preserving radial tangent is a symmetry: ReLU and the linear
  layer commute with an independent positive scale, so the tangent of the state at layer l maps to the tangent of the
  state at layer l + 1 (note XXXII section 4, with its fourth-order part 6 kappa4 + 4 Sym3(Sigma, Sigma) +
  3 Sym4(mu, kappa3)). The Gram-Charlier mean map already satisfies the corresponding cancellation identically.

So the fourth cumulant is slaved: given the pair state, the third-cumulant sources and the scale mode, it has no
independent dynamics at first order. The chain's regenerated core keeps the pair part (assembled incoherently,
note XXXII section 1), replaces L3's omitted classes and the scale mode's omitted classes by one fitted scalar times
C_off, and adjusts that scalar with a rule that is not scale-homogeneous. It contains no D21 dependence in its (3,1)
slice, where the theory puts the leading omitted response, and that slice is the one that is 74-90% wrong.

## 3. One mechanism behind both channels

Both measured channels are gate-facet effects on the carried third-cumulant sources.

- **The third-cumulant readouts (about 40%).** Transport of a source through ReLU replaces E[G_a G_b G_c] by the
  product of marginal gate probabilities. The difference, the gate covariance, is, to leading order in the
  correlations, pairs of facet densities joined by the covariance (phi_a phi_b C_ab / sigma_a sigma_b terms of the
  Mehler series). In the leg representation it is (WA) o (WP) against W(A o P), the treewidth-three contraction that
  costs n^4.
- **The fourth-cumulant sector (most of the rest).** The single-facet third-chaos response L3 generates the omitted
  classes, and the chain does not compute it.

Both are organised by the same object, the common mode of the covariance. In the factor reference
Sigma = diag(s^2) + B B^T, the gates are conditionally independent given X; the gate covariance becomes the covariance
over X of conditional gate probabilities, and the omitted fourth-order classes become outer cumulants over X of the
conditional covariance responses. The cumulant theory's constructive theorem evaluates both at O(nr + n^2) per outer
node and output row, with no three- or four-index array. Note XX (`notes/hidden-separator`) found the same object from
the third-cumulant side: the gate-covariance term is the shadow of a common-mode subspace of dimension about 64 (rank 64
captures 96% of it), and carrying that subspace through the legs costs 64 transports per source and layer, which no
rank repays under the score.

## 4. Why "closed in closed form" was a statement in the wrong norm

Note XXI closed the fourth-order channel to 1% in the gain projection: the projection of the fourth cumulant on
3 sigma^4, weighted toward large-variance neurons. That is one coherent observable. The terminal mean reads the
fourth cumulant through a different family: per neuron, with weight (alpha_i^2 - 1) phi(alpha_i) / (24 sigma_i^3) in
the mean map, and per pair in the pair program, each then carried to the output by the adjoint
v_l = M_l^T v_(l+1). The quotient that the theory prescribes (states equivalent when every future readout agrees)
must be taken in that family. The incoherent per-neuron part of the third-chaos response cancels in the gain
projection and survives in the per-neuron readout. The rule that follows applies to every closure claim: verify it in
the readout-observable norm, never in a coherent summary.

## 5. The frontier, read through the theory

The documented frontier method (Team Puffi's SSC, Phase 1) regenerates the fourth cumulant as
sum_(a,b) Lambda_ab Sym(B_a o B_b) from at most four covariance-response matrices, the algebra of a Gaussian whose
covariance is modulated by background fluctuations. In THEORY_3's terms this is a coherent tensor: every slice comes
from the same product cores. In the response theory's terms its modes are what section 2 derives: covariance responses
to background (common-mode) fluctuations, which the conditional form of L3 produces from the third-cumulant sources,
with the scale mode as the first of them (B = C). Their closing audit found a further 13.6% from fitting the coupling
to the downstream mean correction instead of the marginal fourth cumulant: the readout-observable weighting of
section 4.

This also answers the question the earlier reading left open: how the leaders reach 1.7 times better error at 0.6 of
our flops. The sector oracle is worth -33% alone and much more with the third-cumulant readouts; a coherent,
response-mode fourth-cumulant sector is the natural explanation. It is a hypothesis about a Phase 2 method we have not
seen, consistent with the published Phase 1 one, not a fact.

## 6. What the theory prescribes

**The state.** Keep the mean, the covariance and the third-cumulant legs as they are. Replace the regenerated
fourth-cumulant core by one declared tensor whose constituents are derived, not fitted:

    T_z = W#[ D(d) + S22(K) ]                      the retained pair tensor, transported exactly (note XXXII section 2)
        + 3 g J(C_z, C_z)                          the scale core, carried by the Ward identity
        + sum_(p,q) Lambda_pq J(B_p, B_q)          response cores from the conditional form of L3

with every slice the next layer reads (diagonal, (2,2), (3,1)) taken from T_z. The scale core's slices are free once C is
carried (diagonal 3 g var^2, (2,2) g (var_i var_j + 2 C_ij^2), (3,1) 3 g var_i C_ij). The response matrices are elementwise
in D21 and per-neuron gate functions at the independent reference (d11 above), so forming one costs O(n^2) and
transporting it one n^3 product. Lambda is an outer cumulant of the common-mode law, not a fitted table.

**Two consistency conditions, both exact and Monte Carlo-free.** The assembled map must send the radial tangent of its
state to the radial tangent of its next state (note XXXII section 4), and every declared state must keep each pair's
3 x 3 quadratic-feature Gram matrix positive semidefinite (THEORY_3 section 8).

**The price.** The score is raw x max(0.1, C/B), and the chain sits at C/B of about 0.19. A repair that adds Delta to C/B
pays only if it removes more than Delta / (0.19 + Delta) of the error: 7% for one dense n^3 product per layer, 19% for
three (less under Strassen). The sector is worth 33% alone, so a coherent sector must capture well over half of its
ceiling at about three products per layer, or most of it at fewer. The third-cumulant gate covariance remains the
expensive half: the factor reference makes its coefficients tractable, but carrying them through the legs is the open
cost problem.

**Theory obligations before any run.**

1. The response cores at alpha != 0 and at the correlated reference: closed forms for d11 and its outer cumulants from
   the half-normal moment recursions M_k(a, s) = a M_(k-1) + (k-1) s^2 M_(k-2).
2. The Ward consistency of the assembled map, proved for the scale core and the pair tensor and stated as a condition
   on the response cores.
3. The readout-observable value of each core (its adjoint-weighted Hankel singular value), which fixes how many cores
   to carry, from the reachable-observable theorem rather than by choosing four.
4. Their flopscope price.

Only then the discriminating computations of note XXXII section 6, in its order.
