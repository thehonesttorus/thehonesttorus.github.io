# Prodan's finite-volume programme read against the chain

Working note XVII. Emil Prodan, "A computational non-commutative geometry program for disordered topological
insulators" (arXiv 1611.09737, Springer Briefs 2017), read in its computational core (Chapters 3-6, 8) and its
numerical chapters (7, 9). The question asked was: where is the analogous gold for us. The answer has three parts: a
dictionary (section 2), which says exactly which of Prodan's objects we already have under other names and which we
cannot have; the transferable method (section 3), which is the separation of approximation defects by type with each
type bounded by a decay it is responsible for; and the measurements that the method asks for on the v29 chain
(section 4), two of which are new this session.

## 1. What Prodan builds

- **The infinite object.** Disorder configurations omega in a product space with a translation-invariant, ergodic,
  permutation-invariant probability measure (Assumptions 3.1-3.5; the product measure of iid site disorder is the
  model case). The covariant observables form the twisted crossed product A = C_N(Omega) x| Z^d, generated by
  functions of the environment and translation unitaries u_j. The torus (gauge) action lambda_j u_j gives Fourier
  coefficients a_x in C_N(Omega), derivations d_j a = -i sum_x x_j a_x u_x (the commutators with position in the
  physical representation, (3.28)), and the trace T(a) = integral over omega of tr a_0(omega), which by Birkhoff
  equals the trace per unit volume of pi_omega(a) for almost every omega (3.37). The triple (A, d, T) is the
  non-commutative Brillouin torus; the quantities of physics are correlations T(d^a1 G_1(h) ... d^an G_n(h)).
- **Two quotients.** The periodic algebra (disorder repeated with period 2L+1) and the finite-volume algebra
  (translations on the torus Z^d/(2L+1)) are reached by *-epimorphisms p~ and p^ (Prop 4.10, Lemma 4.20). A
  homomorphism commutes with functional calculus, G(p(h)) = p(G(h)), and the spectrum of the quotient sits inside the
  spectrum of the original: a projective tower adds no spurious spectrum (Remark 5.1), which is why Prodan refuses the
  inductive (embedding) approximations of Pimsner-Voiculescu and Landi-Lizzi-Szabo for topological questions.
- **The one thing that cannot be quotiented.** The finite torus has no generators, so the derivations have no
  image; the canonical approximate derivation d^ a = -i sum_{x in V_L} x_j a_x u^_x is fixed uniquely by agreeing
  with the true one on elements supported in V_L (Remark 4.25). It is not a derivation: the Leibniz rule fails
  (Remark 4.24). Its defect lives only on Fourier coefficients outside V_L (equation 6.29), and that is what the
  error bound uses.
- **Smooth observables.** If the Hamiltonian is in the smooth algebra A^infty (Fourier coefficients decaying faster
  than any power, equivalent to all derivatives bounded, Prop 3.23), which is stable under holomorphic and smooth
  functional calculus (Helffer-Sjostrand almost-analytic extension, Props 3.24-3.25) and under products and
  inverses, and if the coefficients depend on remote disorder with the same rapid decay (a2, stable under products
  and inverses, Prop 6.1), then the finite-volume correlation converges faster than any inverse power of |V_L|
  (Theorems 6.2, 6.4, 6.5). No localization is needed: the smoothness of G supplies the regularity.
- **Non-smooth observables.** For a step function with its jump inside the spectrum there is no norm
  approximation. Theorem 8.4 needs a mobility gap: the Aizenman-Molchanov fractional-moment bound on the resolvent
  (Definition 3.30), holding uniformly in L for the finite approximants (Prop 8.2 proves it for small fluctuations).
  The proof propagates the boundary defect (the finite Hamiltonian differs from the infinite one only on sites within
  the hopping range of the boundary, (8.36)-(8.37)) through three resolvents whose exponential decay kills it, with
  fractional powers s < 1/6 making the |Im z|^-(1-s) singularities of the contour integrals integrable.
- **The algorithm** (5.16-5.17). Represent the finite element on C^N x l^2(V_L), diagonalize, apply G by the
  spectral theorem, apply d^ by the roots-of-unity formula (4.45), average the normalized trace over M independent
  configurations. Self-averaging makes M small in practice (50 configurations on a 140 x 140 lattice, 5 on
  21 x 21 x 21, with the un-averaged winding numbers already quantized to three digits).

## 2. The dictionary

| Prodan | the chain (v29, E10-E11, notes XIV-XVI) |
|---|---|
| disorder omega, iid product measure, permutation invariant | the weights, iid He initialization; the law is invariant under neuron permutations at every layer, so Assumption 3.5 holds exactly, and the finite-width corrections are a finite-size effect of iid disorder, not of correlated disorder |
| translation covariance, Z^d | there is no depth translation (every layer has its own weights) and no spatial structure in a dense layer; the only group is S_n acting on neurons, a symmetry of the law, not of a realization |
| trace T = integral of tr a_0 = trace per unit volume | width-averaged observables (1/n) sum_k of per-neuron quantities; self-averaging in one network to O(n^-1/2); the annealed (mean-field) theory computes them |
| the local coefficient a_0(omega) at one site, one realization | the per-neuron quantities the challenge scores: mu_k(16) for each k; not a trace, not self-averaging; the quenched content of E10 |
| functional calculus G(h) | the gate: the step function (for the pair and cumulant recursions) or ReLU (for the mean) of the pre-activation h |
| smooth G at finite temperature | observables averaged over the Gaussian input: Phi(alpha), phi(alpha) and the Mehler coefficients are smooth in (mu, C); the Gaussian measure is the temperature, and it is exact rather than a smoothing bias |
| the jump of G inside the spectrum, no gap | the gate's jump sits at h = 0 where the pre-activation density phi(alpha)/sigma is largest; there is no gap and no mobility gap |
| Aizenman-Molchanov decay of the response to a remote change | the decay with age of the response of mu(16) to content born at layer b: rho^age with rho = 0.80-0.92 on the kappa_3 channel (E10) and 1 on the mean direction (He criticality makes the pair propagator marginal); the mean direction is the extended state at the Fermi level, and the measured 10% raw per 64 ranks of the shared basis is its flat spectrum |
| finite volume V_L and the two quotients | the carried representation: CP rank, shared-basis rank 384, nested rank 224, age gates 4 and 7, slice-residual rank, feedback rank; all are inductive (subspace embeddings), none is a quotient |
| the canonical d^ with its defect confined outside V_L | the warm-started range finders (note XV): the join's defect is confined to the complement of the transported basis, and we measured that it saturates (a second pass adds nothing, a cold pass loses); the residual leg's defect is its rank tail, measured in section 6 of note XV |
| rapid decay of Fourier coefficients (A^infty) | two decays that are not rapid: the singular values of old-source content in the transported basis (algebraic; the shared basis is not over-provisioned), and the Hermite coefficients of ReLU and of the gate (algebraic, because the gate is not smooth) |
| homomorphic quotients, no spurious spectrum | the exact pieces of the chain are homomorphic: transport nesting P_b'(l) = P_b(l) P_b'(b), sources fixed at birth, A_s = P_s C_b; the Gaussian closure is not a homomorphism: it evaluates G on a surrogate element (a Gaussian with the carried moments), and its defect is the Edgeworth remainder, which E11 showed the fitted kappa_4 diagonal counterterms |
| M samples, self-averaging | the chain is deterministic, as are the leaders' chains; the variance term of Prodan's error budget is zero and the whole error is bias |

Two rows deserve a sentence each. The trace row says Prodan's theorems bound the one thing the challenge does not
score. Every bound in Chapters 6 and 8 is a bound on T, the ensemble-and-space average; the challenge scores
a_0(omega) at every site of one realization, which the trace integrates away. The object we need is the one his
algorithm computes by brute force (represent and diagonalize), which for us is the forward pass the budget forbids.
The mobility-gap row says why the two cheap finite volumes, an age window and a rank gate, cannot have small defects:
assumption c3 fails at He criticality (the frontier measurements, windowed chains six times worse and 10% raw per 64
ranks, are that failure measured), and Theorem 8.4 is the statement that without it the boundary defect of a
non-smooth observable propagates undamped.

## 3. The transferable method

What survives the dictionary is a method, not a theorem.

- **Separate the rounds.** Prodan bounds p~ and p^ separately (Theorems 6.2 and 6.4) and each bound names the decay
  it rests on. The chain has three rounds: closure (Gaussian plus kappa_3 plus the fitted kappa_4 diagonal: the
  model), representation (ranks and gates: the finite volume), and arithmetic (Strassen level, float32). Only the
  third had been measured in isolation (level 6: raw unchanged to four digits). Section 4 measures the second.
- **Confine the defect, then bound it by the decay it lives on.** The approximate derivation is defined so that it is
  exact on everything inside V_L; its error is then a sum over coefficients outside V_L, bounded by their decay. The
  chain's analogue is to define each truncation so that it is exact on the content that has a carrier (the
  transported basis, the mean direction, the frozen core) and to measure the decay of what is left. Where the decay
  is rapid the truncation is free (the residual leg beyond rank 4, section 6 of note XV); where it is algebraic
  (the shared basis) no rank is free and the gate is at its optimum.
- **Smoothness replaces localization.** Theorem 6.5 needs no mobility gap because G is smooth. Our observables
  are smooth in exactly one variable, the carried moments, because the Gaussian input measure integrates the gate.
  So the finite volume in which the chain can converge fast is the cumulant and Mehler order, with the off-diagonal
  correlations of order n^-1/2 as the small parameter, and not depth or rank. The hierarchy must be carried in full
  depth and truncated in order. That is the regime of the leaders' kappa_3-only chains at 0.15 B, and it is the
  reason the 1.7x to them is not in the representation of the old tier (note XV, section 2).
- **Projective, not inductive.** Remark 5.1 prefers quotients of the big algebra over embeddings of small ones
  because quotients cannot invent spectrum. Every compression in the chain is an embedding (a subspace of the
  content). The quotient would be "the content modulo what the readout cannot see": the future layers are known,
  so a readout-weighted sketch for the old-tier range finder is constructible. The warm start was one instance of
  choosing the sketch by theory and it was worth 3% raw; the readout-weighted sketch is the next candidate and is
  not tested here.
- **Self-averaging as a check, not a saving.** T equals the trace per unit volume, so width averages of the chain's
  per-layer quantities must agree with the annealed theory to O(n^-1/2) at every layer. This is a free consistency
  test of the chain's own state (it caught nothing so far because E10 already compared mu, var and the gain against
  Monte Carlo); it saves no flops because the bill is in n^3 transports of non-self-averaging content.

What the method cannot deliver, and the pasted summary of the monograph gets right, is a certificate that a window
or a surrogate is accurate at competition scale: Prodan's certificates are for averages under decay assumptions we
have measured to be false at the critical point. The Gaussian conditional Poincare bound in that summary is a
certificate for a different estimator family (an integrable surrogate plus a Monte Carlo residual), whose matched-cost
losses the frontier digest already records; the chain is the surrogate with the residual set to zero.

## 4. Measurements the method asked for

**Round two in isolation: the representation defect of v29.** Run the chain with the shared basis off
(`V21_NO_CONFINE=1`, every source at full rank) and, in a second run, with the residual and feedback ranks raised to
64, both under a diagnostic budget (`code/run_v29diag.py`, 2^44 FLOPs so the meter does not stop the run), with the
warm feedback sketch on. The difference to the production chain is the whole finite-volume defect; what remains
between the uncompressed chain and the truth is closure.

| chain (network 0, warm feedback sketch) | raw | C/B | raw against production |
|---|---|---|---|
| production v29 + warm join + warm feedback (shared basis 384, nested 224, residual 16, feedback 16) | 2.183e-8 | 0.267 | 0 |
| no shared basis: every source dense at full rank | 2.153e-8 | 0.312 | -1.4% |
| no shared basis, residual rank 64, feedback rank 64 | 2.125e-8 | 0.380 | -2.7% |

The whole representation defect of v29 is 2.7% of its raw error, bought for 30% of the bill. The shared-basis tier
(rank 384 after age 4, nested 224 after age 7) costs 1.4% raw and saves 17% of the bill; the two thin legs cost 1.3%
raw for the remaining 8%. Read with the rank slope of note XV, section 6 (10% raw per 64 ranks below 384, 1.4% for
all 640 ranks above it), the old-source content has a knee at rank about 384: it is the Lyapunov spectrum of the
propagators, whose products of gated weights have about n/3 singular values above the floor after four or more
layers. So there is a decay in rank space, it is sharp, and v29's gate sits exactly on it. In Prodan's terms the
finite-volume round is converged; everything that separates the chain from the truth, 97% of its error and the
whole 1.7x to the leaders, is closure. The remaining programme is therefore the closure (the Edgeworth remainder of
the gate, the Mehler order of the pair recursion, which `est_v29.py` truncates at second order in the off-diagonal
covariance, and the off-diagonal fourth cumulant that the fitted diagonal counterterms), not the representation.
Note XV's section 5 wins (warm starts, level 6, residual rank 4) are what the representation round had left.

**The residual leg's rank tail** is recorded in note XV, section 6: raw flat to +-1.5% from rank 4 to 32, rank 2
worse by 3.4%, so the content above the defect floor is three or four directions, and rank 4 with warm starts and
level 6 is the combined best (two-network mean adjusted 5.47e-9, -6.3% against the shipped chain).
